Education Settings Information Tool (ESIT)

This Education Settings Information Tool (ESIT) shows the distribution of settings across the London Borough of Hackney. The data contained for each setting is publicly available information and is designed to be used to inform and support users in understanding the make-up and context of the settings in their respective localities. The ESIT is also designed to serve as a transparent resource to inform decision-making for schools, the LA and wider stakeholders.
